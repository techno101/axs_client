# Independent visual review

Decision: **REVISE**
Recorded: 2026-08-02
Reviewer: independent read-only visual-verifier agent
Scope: Checkpoint 1 pre-production pack, four low-resolution anchors, contact sheet and typography/palette proof

The pre-production gate remains closed. No high-resolution batch generation or homepage component coding is authorized.

## What is working

- The Match Cut direction, eight-chapter narrative, desktop/mobile wireframes and unique-role asset plan are coherent.
- Native fallbacks, accessibility behavior and reduced-motion rules are specified coherently.
- The restrained night palette is legible and recognizably derived from the ArmourX blue and green.
- The anchors retain useful Malaysian community-ground scale; no foreign stadium appears.
- Main action physics are generally credible, especially in `M08`, with no obvious catastrophic extra limbs.

## Blocking visual findings

1. The opening-to-hero transformation is not visually proven. `M01` and `M03` share no stable player, ball, crop or floodlight invariant, so the contact sheet cuts to a different scene instead of visibly becoming the hero.
2. Brand cleanliness fails. `M01` and `M03` retain crest-like chest marks; `M13` contains multiple swoosh/product-like footwear details.
3. Venue continuity is inconsistent. `M13` changes the real rectangular floodlight arrays to six round lamps, while `M08` becomes a generic fenced pitch with a single featureless light.
4. Production cleanup is still required for small background faces/bodies, soft hand/finger interactions, footwear and `M01`'s crowded duel/ball details.
5. The typography proof does not demonstrate Exo 2 ExtraBold/Black as the proposed display fallback: the campaign headline reads light/regular. It also does not prove long BM copy, mobile wrapping or 200% zoom.
6. BM remains a draft. `Jejak ke padang` requires native-language review.
