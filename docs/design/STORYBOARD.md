# Eight-chapter homepage storyboard

Status: `REVISED FOR VERIFICATION`
Concept: The Match Cut
Key line: **Book your spot.**
Asset target: 16 unique still masters, one required hero clip, no more than one supporting clip

This replaces every visible v12 homepage line. It removes the rejected daylight-versus-floodlights story, the six-hour sales pitch, the field-ownership slogan, the twin-field sales section, invented facility copy and the generic pricing/card flow.

## Voice

- Short, direct and useful.
- Football first. No begging, cinematic poetry or fake professional-stadium language.
- The venue is shown through real geometry and real booking information, not exaggerated claims.
- **Book your spot** is the only English booking label used in navigation, hero, booking entry and final action.
- Bahasa Melayu uses **Tempah slot anda**.

## Frame-by-frame contract

| Beat | Visual | Transformation | Asset |

| Hero | The exact same player, ball position and rectangular floodlight array expand into the hero | A visible invariant proves the Match Cut | `M03` hero anchor |

1. `BOOK YOUR SPOT.`
2. `Football in Iskandar Puteri. Pick a date, choose an available pitch, and get your game booked.`

No second CTA, pricing teaser, availability strip, field claim or atmospheric slogan appears in the hero.

2. Is every line direct, useful and free of the rejected daylight, six-hour, field-ownership and facility language?
3. Is **Book your spot** the only English booking action label?
4. Does each dominant image have exactly one role?
5. Are verified venue hardware and Johor surroundings consistent across every anchor?
6. Does the page remain complete with native scroll, failed media, failed JavaScript and reduced motion?
