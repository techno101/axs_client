# Reference study, not copying

Status: `REVISE`
Captured: 2026-08-02
Method: current public HTML/accessibility-tree evidence, source-site metadata and headless desktop/mobile captures

No source text, media, logo, layout, animation or code is licensed for reuse. The observations below are principles to test against the ArmourXSports brief, not a recipe to reproduce another site.

## Source-by-source takeaways

### United, Football and the 2026 World Cup

Source: <https://united-in-football.framer.website/>

Observed:

- a single editorial thesis carries a long page;
- recurring kinetic type and repeated thematic language make chapters feel connected;
- large media sequences are grouped around emotion rather than a component catalogue;
- sound is offered as a control, not assumed.

Apply:

- one direct key line, `Book your spot`, should anchor the hero, booking entry and final action without additional campaign slogans;
- chapter labels and match vocabulary can create continuity without decorative geometry;
- the contact-sheet language should remain recognizable after the hero.

Deliberately do not copy:

- World Cup scale, global statistics, country tables, superstar/player narratives, typography treatment, copy, sound or imagery;
- unsupported emotional/historical claims.

### The Performance Lab

Source: <https://theperformancelab.ca/>

Observed:

- strong media density is balanced by explicit service/process information;
- numbered sequences and changing media rhythm communicate depth;
- credibility content sits close to the related service decision.

Apply:

- keep real session/price/location facts close to the booking decision;
- change composition between chapters without changing the underlying design language;
- make chapter numbers a reading aid, not a decorative score graphic.

Deliberately do not copy:

- its claims, service numbering, laboratory language, layouts, media, image stacks or testimonial structure;
- its repetitive service-card treatment.

### Powerleague and its booking redesign

Sources:

- <https://www.awwwards.com/sites/powerleague>
- <https://www.plugandplaydesign.co.uk/powerleague/>
- <https://www.powerleague.com/about-us>

Observed:

- the case study prioritizes reducing steps from search to purchase and showing times/prices in a comparable calendar;
- current community photography makes small-sided football feel social and real;
- useful booking rules belong inside the booking flow, not in decorative marketing UI.

Apply:

- let the cinematic story resolve into real, comparable Admin-backed availability;
- show policy and support at the commitment point;
- keep ArmourXSports community scale visible rather than manufacturing a stadium.

Deliberately do not copy:

- Powerleague brand language, shapes, imagery, facility claims, location search, membership benefits or booking constraints;
- any `world-class`, LED or surface language that is not proved for ArmourXSports.

### BVB Tribute

Source: <https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bvb-tribute>

Observed:

- a strict two-colour identity and historical editorial frame create a memorable single-page story;
- parallax/microinteraction is subordinate to the fan narrative;
- desktop and mobile presentations are treated as related but distinct compositions.

Apply:

- use ArmourXSports blue/green/night neutrals consistently;
- author a separate touch rhythm rather than scaling desktop pinning;
- let motion serve a chapter change.

Deliberately do not copy:

- Dortmund history, black/yellow palette, fan-tribute language, archival media, layout or parallax treatment.

### Toulouse Football Club Play and official site

Sources:

- <https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/play-toulouse-football-club-1>
- <https://www.toulousefc.com/>

Observed:

- media, video, podcast and social formats are treated as a coherent club content system;
- match information, teams, shop, venue and community commitments remain discoverable despite a rich visual identity;
- the current official page uses explicit labels and practical routes rather than hiding utility behind motion.

Apply:

- protect booking, language, contact and venue routes while the homepage becomes cinematic;
- label content types and data clearly;
- use a match-frame transition only when it leads to useful content.

Deliberately do not copy:

- club editorial modules, match centre, violet identity, media assets, scores, history or social feed.

### 36Five X Escape

Source: <https://escape.36fivex.com/>

Observed:

- direct facility and event actions appear early;
- brief text establishes a local experience business without forcing a large visual metaphor;
- availability absence is stated plainly instead of replaced with fake content.

Apply:

- retain early `Book your spot` access and honest empty/error states;
- give location and booking facts direct language.

Deliberately do not copy:

- its company, facilities, event data, booking rules, copy, layouts or visual assets. Its full-size/track claims belong only to its venue and must never migrate to ArmourXSports.

### Current Apple Malaysia and Apple motion guidance

Sources:

- <https://www.apple.com/my/>
- <https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion>
- <https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility/>

Observed:

- one product/idea dominates each visual field;
- CTA hierarchy remains explicit even when product media is highly composed;
- Apple guidance asks motion to be purposeful, optional and responsive to reduced-motion settings.

Apply:

- keep one dominant idea per chapter and preserve visible actions;
- make every full-screen/scroll animation interruptible;
- replace reduced-motion transforms with immediate composition or a short cross-fade.

Deliberately do not copy:

- product staging, white-space ratios, device arrangements, Liquid Glass, type, copy or proprietary transitions.

### Current Nike Malaysia football

Source: <https://www.nike.com/my/w/football-1gdj0>

Observed:

- football is communicated through decisive, tightly cropped action and a direct shopping path;
- a single action image can carry more energy than a dense decorative layout;
- filters and product decisions stay straightforward.

Apply:

- demand one readable action per `Inside the game` master;
- protect the booking path from the cinematic layer.

Deliberately do not copy:

- athletes, products, campaign lines, Swoosh, image crops, retail UI, team kits or product claims.

### Current adidas Malaysia football

Source: <https://www.adidas.com.my/en/football>

Observed:

- one short idea distinguishes each football family/campaign;
- men and women appear across athlete/product storytelling;
- surface/product categories are explicit where the customer must decide.

Apply:

- keep one campaign idea for ArmourXSports and distinct fictional adult community scenarios;
- keep authoritative pitch selection and rules in the task flow.

Deliberately do not copy:

- athlete identities, club/national kits, World Cup material, three-stripe identity, product slogans, carousel layout or surface claims.

## Synthesis for ArmourXSports

The useful common thread is disciplined hierarchy: a strong idea can support rich media only when booking, place and accessibility remain explicit. The ArmourXSports distinction is the real two-pitch Johor ground and its local community scale. The site should not compete with references through louder effects; it should make its own place and booking action more specific.

The chosen synthesis is:

- contact-sheet opening as hero, never loader;
- one match-cut grammar across eight chapters;
- unique local/reference-guided media rather than stock or foreign stadiums;
- real Admin data at the narrative turning point;
- native mobile/task flows and purposeful reduced-motion behavior;
- no copied layout, source code, media, brand or claim.
